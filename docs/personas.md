# User Personas

> These are draft personas. Replace the details with what you learn from real people you speak to.

## 1. Kawira: diaspora parent

- **Who:** 38, nurse living in the UK; grew up in Meru, moved abroad 12 years ago.
- **Goal:** wants her two children (7 and 10) to learn to count and greet their grandparents in Kimeru.
- **Frustration:** her children only hear Kimeru on phone calls home. Most apps she finds teach Swahili, not Kimeru.
- **Needs from the site:** short, playful lessons on a phone; audio would be a bonus; a number quiz the kids can do on their own.

## 2. Mr. Mutuma: lower-primary teacher in Meru

- **Who:** 45, teaches lower primary in Imenti; teaches indigenous-language lessons.
- **Goal:** quick, free digital practice he can show on a projector or share with parents.
- **Frustration:** few printed or digital Kimeru materials; children from town homes often speak Swahili/English at home.
- **Needs from the site:** clear word lists by topic (numbers, foods, greetings), simple arithmetic drills, works on low bandwidth.

## 3. Brian: university student with Meru roots

- **Who:** 21, IT student in Nairobi; his parents are from Meru, but he grew up speaking Swahili and English.
- **Goal:** to understand his grandparents and not feel left out at family gatherings.
- **Frustration:** he understands a little but can't count or name foods; he's embarrassed to ask.
- **Needs from the site:** self-paced, private practice; a score history so he can see progress.
